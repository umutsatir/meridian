Statistics and sound

Data here is a personal log, not a scoreboard. One accent, no rainbow charts, big serif numbers. The sound mixer sits next to it because both are about the quality of time spent: what you did, and what it sounded like.

Bars are stacked by scenario in the scenario tints, so one glance shows what the time was spent on. Hovering a bar lifts it to full strength and opens a tooltip with the per-scenario breakdown. Day is hours, week is days, month is weeks, year is months. The axis is 11 pt Mono in the third text tone and there are no gridlines except the baseline.

Sound mixer and announcements

Default · preset “Deep focus”
Keyboard focus on the third layer
Rules
Seven ambient layers (cabin hum, rain on glass, ocean, forest, brown noise, pink noise, soft music), each 0–100 %. Presets set all levels at once and glide over 600 ms. Announcements are a separate section so the voice never fights the noise.
Every control has a visible state. Sliders step by 5 with arrow keys and by 25 with Option. Mute is a single switch that holds the levels so un-muting returns to the same mix.
NativeLayers are AVAudioEngine player nodes mixed into one bus with a shared limiter. Presets are Codable structs; the sliders are Slider with a custom track. Announcements use AVSpeechSynthesizer with a bundled chime.
