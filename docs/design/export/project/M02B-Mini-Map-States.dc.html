Release within 56 pt of a corner and the window glides to a 20 pt margin. Hold ⌥ to move freely.
NativeNSPanel (.nonactivatingPanel, .borderless), level .floating, collectionBehavior [.canJoinAllSpaces, .fullScreenAuxiliary]. Content: SwiftUI with .glassEffect(.regular, in: .rect(cornerRadius: 20)). Snap: NSAnimationContext with a spring timing function on setFrame(display:animate:).
HoverNSTrackingArea (.mouseEnteredAndExited, .activeAlways). Controls fade and rise 6 pt in 180 ms; they hide again 600 ms after the pointer leaves. A focused control also reveals the row, so the keyboard never loses it.
Click-throughignoresMouseEvents = true. The window shows a “Click-through” pill and cannot be moved. Turn it off from the menu bar popover or with ⌥⌘M, because the window itself can no longer be clicked.
VoiceOver“Mini map, 18 minutes to Paris, group.” Controls: Pause, Sound, Size, Close, each a button with its current value. Reduce Motion: no rise on hover, instant snap.
Reduce TransparencyGlass border stays, map layer stays opaque; the control buttons switch to solid bg-2.
