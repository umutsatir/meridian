Ticket printing

A 2.4 s mechanical ritual on a dim, blurred map. A small printer sits at the bottom of the stage and the boarding pass is pushed up out of its slit, leading edge first, like a receipt from a kiosk. Paper steps out fourteen times, each step a quick ease-out and a short hold, so it stutters like a real thermal head. The newest ink is pale and warm at the slit and darkens to black as it rises. Space or Return fast-forwards. With Reduce Motion on there is no printer: the pass cross-fades in at rest over 200 ms with one soft tick and no haptic steps. The live version is prototype P1.

Each step: easeOutCubic over 84 ms from k/14 to (k+1)/14, then 30 ms hold. 14 × 114 ms = 1,596 ms, which fills the 200–1,800 ms window. The warm development veil rides the slit and is 120 pt tall; the pass is clipped at the slit’s near lip, so its trailing end always looks tucked inside the printer.
