RulesButtons keep their width and let the label shrink to 12 pt before it ellipsizes. Pills, chips and times never wrap and grow to the right. Headlines wrap to two lines at most. Every container is laid out with HStack and VStack, never fixed offsets, so SwiftUI’s own 30 % expansion test needs no changes.
Right to leftNot designed here, but nothing blocks it: icons that point (chevrons, arrows) are mirrored with .flipsHorizontallyInRightToLeft, the pass, perforation and ticket numbers are not mirrored because they are objects, and the map is never mirrored.
