A01 · Sound specs

Nine synthesized sounds

Nothing is sampled. Every effect is a few oscillators and filtered noise, so the whole set weighs a few kilobytes, can be tuned in code and always agrees with the animation it belongs to. Press a play button to hear the exact recipe from the prototypes.
Envelopes are drawn from the real recipe values. Frequency bars use a log axis, 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
UI sounds
Taps, tears and stamps. Off silences every effect except announcements.
Announcements
Seatbelt, boarding and landing chimes. Always paired with a banner or a label, never sound-only.
Mix bus
Master 0.85 into a compressor (threshold −16 dB, 3 : 1). One shared 1.3 s room: decaying stereo noise impulse, per-sound send 0.35–0.55. The stamp thump is the loudest effect (peak .55); the printer is the softest texture (.045).

AHow the set was tuned
Short and lowEffects are under 400 ms except the printer and the chimes. Most of their energy sits below 4 kHz, so nothing is shrill after an hour of use.
One roomEvery sound that has a tail shares the same 1.3 s room, so the set sounds like one cabin and not a sound pack.
A visual twinEach sound lines up with a motion on T01 to T03 and with a haptic. Nothing is announced by sound alone.
No loopsOnly the ambient mixer layers (C10) loop. Effects never repeat on their own and never play in the background.
TunableEvery number on this sheet is a constant in one file, so a sound designer can change the set without touching the UI.
NativeOne AVAudioEngine for the app. Each effect is rendered once at launch into an AVAudioPCMBuffer from the same recipes (oscillators, filtered noise, exponential envelopes) and played from AVAudioPlayerNodes, so latency stays low. The room is an AVAudioUnitReverb (small room) on a send bus; the master bus ends in an AVAudioUnitDynamicsProcessor. Announcement chimes are also bundled as 1.2 s .caf files for notifications.
RulesEffects follow the “UI sounds” toggle and the macOS setting “Play user interface sound effects”. Announcements follow their own toggle and Focus. Sound is never the only signal: every sound here has a visual twin and, on a trackpad, a haptic from NSHapticFeedbackManager (alignment, level change or generic). Everything follows the system output volume and mute.
