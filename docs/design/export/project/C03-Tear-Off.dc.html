Tear-off stub

The star moment. Grab the stub, pull along the perforation, feel it resist, then let go of control: past 72% the paper finishes the tear by itself, flies into the Wallet icon and the ticket slides up into the gate. Under 72% the partial tear stays and you can pick it back up. Try it for real in prototype P1.

Same mechanic, one ticket per passenger
When the gate opens each friend checks in; their stub tears with the same rip, flies to their own corner of the lobby and the passenger strip lights their avatar. Order is check-in order, 400 ms apart, so a group of six finishes in 2.4 s. Your own stub is always interactive, theirs play as animation.
NativeOne TearModel per ticket (progress, grab side, velocity); remote passengers drive it from a timeline instead of a DragGesture.
