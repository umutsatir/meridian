Map styles

Four ways to look at the same sky: Monochrome, Terra, Standard and Satellite, plus a Labels toggle and a dot-matrix world for tickets and widgets. Each is a set of five tokens, so the whole app reskins with the map and glass stays legible on every one.

Labels toggle

Labels on
Sea names in italic caps, cities in sentence case, 11 pt, 46% white. Labels thin out as you zoom out; collisions are resolved by city size.
Labels off
Pure shape. Airport tags and your route are the only text left, which is the point of the focus view.
Control
Labels
Show both names for disputed places
Labels default to English or your system language; disputed places show one name unless you ask for both. Never a hamburger, always a plain toggle.

Dot-matrix world

Dark · widget and ticket variant
Ink on paper · boarding-pass watermark
Dot spacing is world-width ÷ 220, dot radius 38% of the cell. The same mask drives the ticket watermark, the widgets and the Year in flight card.
NativeEach style is a MapStyle plus a Metal color-ramp overlay: .imagery for Satellite with an edge-darkening gradient so glass stays legible, a custom MKMapConfiguration for Terra and Monochrome. Dot-matrix is a Metal compute pass over the land mask.
