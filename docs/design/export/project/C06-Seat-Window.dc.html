Seat map and window view

Seat choice is the calm part of the ritual. A top-down airliner in dark glass: a slim capsule fuselage with a long rounded nose and two-pane cockpit glazing, 2-2 seating (A C | D F) either side of a centre aisle that carries the row numbers, rows 01 to 12, faint swept wings and a plain round tail cap. The cabin is shortened for the screen and the wings fade out toward the tips. The zone you tap sets the scenario, so a seat is a decision and not a decoration. A window seat previews the window it will give you.

Window view

Always dark cabin wall, both themes

A rounded-rectangle cabin window, pane aspect 0.7. A near-black sidewall with fabric grain and a vignette; a thick dark-gray moulded trim that fades into it; a light off-white reveal with a bevel and a thin dark ring; a shade housing that overlaps the top edge with a light pull slit. Through the pane: an oblique, hazy aerial city view with a pale sky band, the far horizon, towers on the skyline, a long park and a landmark in the middle distance. The shade drags like a real one.
