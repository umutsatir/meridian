Passport and stamps

Paper in a glass app. The passport is the one place Meridian looks like an object on a desk: two cream pages, a dark spine, and ink that was clearly pressed by hand. Each stamp is original, in the scenario’s ink, rotated up to ±12° and slightly bled. Pages turn the way paper does: grab the outer edge with the mouse and pull it across the spine.

Stamping animation

{{f.n}}
Sound: a low “thunk”, 90 Hz body with a short noise burst, 120 ms, played once per stamp; a staggered sequence of several stamps plays at 140 ms intervals. Haptic: a firm single tap. Reduce Motion replaces thump and bleed with a 200 ms fade.

Turning pages

{{f.t}}
{{f.n}}
Grab and release
Grab zones are the outer 54 pt of each page, full height. Hover or keyboard focus lifts a dog-ear at the bottom corner (18 → 46 pt, 160 ms) and the cursor becomes an open hand; pressing makes it a closed hand. While dragging, the free edge follows the pointer and the leaf angle is acos((x − spine) ÷ 486), so the paper never stretches. Release past the spine (90°) or with a flick faster than 0.5 pt/ms from 15° finishes the turn in 380 ms; anything less settles back in 260 ms. Only the leaf and the page it uncovers are drawn during a turn.
Light and shadow
The turning leaf darkens toward its free edge by up to 42%, with a 14% warm lift on the rising face. The page it uncovers receives a cast shadow from the spine, up to 36%, peaking at 90°. Perspective is 2400 pt from a point on the spine, so the leaf foreshortens without looking fisheyed.
Other ways to turn
Return, space or the left and right arrow keys on a focused edge play the same 520 ms turn. VoiceOver reads each edge as “Turn to the next spread” and announces “Page 16, Visas, 6 stamps” on landing. Reduce Motion drops the turn: the spread swaps at once (a 200 ms cross-fade in the native build) and drag is disabled.

Year is a segmented control; scenario is a row of chips that multi-select. Filtering turns the page and re-places stamps with a Smooth spring; “3 stamps shown” is announced to VoiceOver.
NativeStamp art is vector with a Metal ink shader (noise-thresholded alpha plus a 0.3 pt bleed blur); pages are two rotation3DEffect leaves (anchor .leading / .trailing, perspective 0.4) driven by a DragGesture on the page edge, with a spring-settled release. Stamping is a KeyframeAnimator: scale 1.45→0.98 in 60 ms, 1.05 bleed in 140 ms, settle 1.0.
