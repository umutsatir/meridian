Boarding pass

The hero artifact. 360 × 600 pt of warm thermal paper, radius 22, perforation at y = 450 with 14 pt notches. The paper is the only light object in the dark product, so it always reads as the thing you are holding. Thermal ink #14110F at 92%, a 0.3 px bleed copy at 20%, 3% grain, 6% top sheen.
