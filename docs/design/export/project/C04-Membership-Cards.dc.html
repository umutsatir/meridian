Membership cards

Six tiers, six materials, one rule: every card is a small physical object. ID-1 ratio 1.586, 340 × 214 pt in lists and 480 × 303 pt in the hero carousel, radius 20. Pointer-driven 3D tilt with perspective 900, a specular glare that follows the pointer, foil that shifts hue with the angle, parallax layers at z = 0 / 12 / 24 / 36. The tilting prototype is P2.
