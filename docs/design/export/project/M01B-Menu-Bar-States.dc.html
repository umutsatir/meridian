NativeMenuBarExtra with .menuBarExtraStyle(.window). The label is a SwiftUI view in an NSStatusItem: Image(systemName:) template plus Text with .monospacedDigit(), refreshed by TimelineView(.periodic(every: 1)). Popover glass = .glassEffect(.regular, in: .rect(cornerRadius: 22)).
VoiceOver“Meridian, in flight, 42 minutes 18 seconds left, button.” The seconds are announced only when the popover is open, so the menu bar never chatters.
Keys⌥⌘P pause or resume · ⌥⌘L hold to land early · ⌥⌘M mini map. The popover closes on Esc and when you click outside.
RulesNo red in the menu bar, ever. Last minute pulses once every 1.4 s with a 4 pt accent ring; Reduce Motion replaces the pulse with a steady ring. Amber dot plus the time means boarding, never the dot alone.
Reduce TransparencyThe popover fills with solid bg-2 and a hairline; the arrow keeps its 6 pt notch.
